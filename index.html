The Lindy Effect & Architectural Durability

The Lindy Effect states that the future life expectancy of a non-perishable technology or idea is proportional to its current age. The longer it has survived, the longer it will continue to survive.

Architectural Rule: In high-stakes software engineering, 80% of your stack should be built on Lindy-proven primitives (Unix, SQL, HTTP) that are 10+ years old. Confine novelty to the experimental 20% edge.
